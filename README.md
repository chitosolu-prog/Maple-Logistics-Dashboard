# Maple-Logistics Sales Dashboard

##Project overview

This excel project analyses a three-year operational dataset from Maple Logistics, a shipping company operating across the United States.

The objective was to evaluate sales performance, profitability trends, customer activity and operational efficiency to identify opportunity for business growth and strategic decision making. The analysis was conducted as part of a data analytics project completed through Amdor Analytics.

##Tools & Techniques Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Data Cleaning and Transformation
- Data Visualization 
- Business Performance Analysis

  ##Key Performance Indicators(KPIs)

 - Total Sales $1.81M
  - Total Profit $236.9K
  - Total Orders 8,001
  - Total Customers 790
  - Product Sold 1,823

  ##Key Insights
  - Identified technology as the top performing category based on average profit.
  - Some categories received substantial discounts but generated low profit, indicating potential inefficiencies in pricing and promotional strategies.
  - Some products generated lower profit margin in certain state compared to others, indicating regional difference in product performance and profitability.

##Recommendations
- Review pricing and discount strategies in states with low product profitability.
- Focus marketing and sales efforts on high margin products in each state.
- Develop state specific strategies to improve profitability and product performance.
